How it's built

Who's on the roster

Every player on today's roster is rated with Box Plus/Minus from Basketball-Reference (last few seasons, this season weighted most). The projected rotation's minutes-weighted rating is the team's strength. Before the season it's blended 50/50 with sportsbook win totals.

How they're playing

A random forest trained on this season's results only: recent scoring, shooting, defense, rest and home/away record. It counts for nothing on opening night and about half after 20 games, so last year's team doesn't decide this year's games.

Injuries & odds

Injured players' minutes go to the rest of the roster and the rating is recomputed, so losing a star costs more than losing a bench player. Win probabilities come from the predicted margin and how far off past predictions have been.
